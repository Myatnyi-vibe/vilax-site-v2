VILAX Support

Help with the VILAX apps, the web version and connections to ChatGPT or Codex

VILAX app for iPhone and iPad

  1. Sign in with the account your company created for you: Apple, email or Telegram.
  2. To sign in with Face ID, open Settings → Account → Sign-in methods and add Face ID.
  3. To get alerts, allow notifications when the app asks, or later in iOS Settings → VILAX.
  4. To delete your account, open Settings → Account → Delete account. Deletion is completed after 30 days, and you can cancel it before then.

Connecting ChatGPT or Codex

Once the plugin is published:

  1. Install VILAX from the available OpenAI plugin directory.
  2. Click connect account and sign in to VILAX.
  3. Review the consent screen and confirm access.
  4. Ask: “Show the VILAX projects available to me”.

The plugin never asks you to copy an API token by hand. The data available to you is defined by your VILAX role.

If data does not load

  1. Disconnect VILAX in your OpenAI connection settings.
  2. Connect it again and complete the OAuth sign-in.
  3. Name the project or location if you have several.
  4. Send support the time of the error and the trace ID shown; never send a password or token.

How to revoke access

Remove the VILAX connection in your OpenAI settings. A VILAX administrator can also revoke a role or project access. After revocation, new requests for restricted data are not run.

Contact us

Email support@vilax.ai. Include your account email, the product (iPhone/iPad app, web, ChatGPT or Codex), the time of the problem and the trace ID. Never send passwords, sign-in codes or bearer tokens.
