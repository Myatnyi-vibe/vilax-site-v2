VILAX Privacy Policy

Effective 22 September 2026, updated 5 October 2026

This policy explains how VILAX processes data on the website app.vilax.ai, in the VILAX and VILAX Team mobile apps for iPhone and iPad, and when VILAX is connected to ChatGPT and Codex through OAuth and the Model Context Protocol (MCP). VILAX is developed by a team based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, and processes personal data in line with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL).

What data we use

After you sign in, we process your account identifier and contact details, the projects and locations you have been granted, and the metrics you request. Which financial and operational data you can see is defined by your VILAX permissions and checked on every request.

For security and diagnostics we keep limited technical events: user and OAuth client identifiers, the name of the tool called, time, duration, result and trace ID. Access tokens, passwords and full financial responses are not written to routine technical logs.

Purposes and sharing

Data is used to sign you in, run your requests, enforce access rights, prevent abuse, provide support and improve reliability. When you use VILAX from ChatGPT or Codex, the answer to your request is passed to the connected OpenAI client so that the model you chose can respond. Infrastructure providers process data only to the extent needed to run VILAX. We do not sell personal data.

VILAX mobile apps

The apps show the same VILAX as the website and additionally process:

  • Sign in with Apple: a private Apple identifier and, if you allow it, your name and email;
  • a push-notification token for your device, only if you allow notifications;
  • voice, only when you tap the microphone button to ask the AI Coordinator a question: the recording is sent to our transcription provider (Groq), and VILAX does not store it;
  • camera photos, only when you choose to take a photo and attach it;
  • Face ID sign-in: biometrics stay on your device, VILAX stores only the public part of your sign-in key.

Questions to the AI Coordinator, together with the metrics needed to answer them, are processed by our AI provider (Anthropic). The apps use no advertising identifiers and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. You can delete your account in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Deletion is completed after a 30-day grace period, during which you can cancel the request.

Your control

You can revoke access in your VILAX or OpenAI connection settings. An administrator can change project and location permissions; a new restriction applies from the next request. To access, correct or delete your data, write to support@vilax.ai. Under the PDPL you may also request restriction or portability of your data, object to processing and withdraw consent. Deletion requests are handled subject to mandatory record-keeping and security requirements.

Retention and security

Data is kept as long as needed to provide the service, meet contractual and legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect the system. We use access control by role, encrypted connections, audit trails and server-side OAuth and permission checks. Some providers may process data outside the UAE under appropriate safeguards.

Requests are stored in the VILAX customer records system and are visible only to managers of your region; team notifications about a new request contain neither your name nor your phone number. Website analytics run only after you accept them in the cookie banner.

Analytics and cookies on vilax.ai

Draft section (R-11), to be reviewed by a lawyer.

The vilax.ai website may use the web analytics service Yandex Metrica (Yandex LLC, Russia). The counter turns on only after you click “Allow” in the cookie banner. Until then the site does not load the counter and makes no requests to Yandex servers. Session replay (Webvisor) and click maps are not used.

What is collected after you allow it: the page you open and the page you came from, campaign tags (UTM), device type, operating system and browser, screen size, language, approximate region based on the IP address, visit time, and a pseudonymous visitor ID stored in Metrica cookies. We only see aggregated reports and site events: version choice, “Request a demo” clicks and the section they came from, opening and submitting the form, calculator use, and viewing prices. Your name, phone number and other form fields are never sent to Metrica.

Why: to understand which sections and sources lead to demo requests and to improve the site. The legal basis is your consent. Metrica cookies stay in your browser for up to [1 year]; retention of report data follows Yandex rules [to be confirmed]. To withdraw consent, open “Cookie settings” in the site footer and choose “Decline”: the counter stops loading, and cookies already stored can be deleted in your browser settings. Yandex privacy policy: yandex.com/legal/confidential.

Without your consent the site stores only what it needs to work: the site version you chose (Russia or English), your answer in the cookie banner, and a draft of the demo request form until you close the tab.
